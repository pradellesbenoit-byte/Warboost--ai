WarBoost V2.5.28 HF8.6.4 — installation guide

Base required:
- WarBoost V2.5.28 HF8.6.3 already deployed on public-beta-safe-launch.

Use:
- WarBoost_V2.5.28_HF8.6.4_RANK_PERSISTENCE_RELIABILITY_PATCH.zip

Steps:
1. Do not change Supabase.
2. Decompress the PATCH.
3. Copy the PATCH contents to the repository root of branch public-beta-safe-launch, replacing files with the same names.
4. Commit/push the changes.
5. Wait until the Vercel Preview deployment is Ready.
6. Test on the Preview URL first.
7. Test one R4→R3 change. After confirmation, the row and counters must update immediately.
8. Refresh/reopen the Preview. The changed grade must still be present.
9. Then test a balanced R4↔R3 swap and one R1↔R2 change.

Expected result for the reported bug:
- After confirming one R4→R3, the screen must no longer remain visually at R4→R4.
- The counters update immediately (example: R4 10→9, R3 69→70).
- Reloading/syncing must not restore the old grade.

Do not deploy to main/Production until the Preview tests pass.
