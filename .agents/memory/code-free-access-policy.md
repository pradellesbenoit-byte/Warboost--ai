Players on every Last War server/alliance, including players without an alliance, must be able to use personal FREE functions after email confirmation and privacy consent, without a beta code, sponsor, invitation or administrator approval.

**Why:** The owner explicitly replaced the private-beta access policy and wants autonomous player registration. This supersedes the alliance-required part of the older onboarding policy.

**How to apply:** Keep personal account access separate from alliance membership, managerial authority and PRO. Preserve historical beta PRO grants, saved data and sessions; never grant PRO or R4/R5 simply by making FREE access available. Alliance membership is scoped by server and tag, with verified identity/manager approval. A self-declared rank or self-authored roster is not independent proof for bootstrapping ownership. Payments remain OFF/TEST; the planned monthly price is not a sale authorization.

Without independent Last War role proof, allow autonomous new WarBoost workspaces governed by their WarBoost founder, with roster preparation and membership requests/approval. This authority is not a verified in-game R4/R5 rank and must never authorize takeover of an existing alliance or its private data.

**Why:** The owner explicitly expanded the provisional-draft approach to practical autonomous governance of a new empty space, without requiring WarBoost administrator intervention or claiming official Last War rank verification.

**How to apply:** Keep founder-space permissions distinct from existing canonical alliance authority. Clearly label declared game ranks as unverified; approval grants access only to the founder's own space, never another alliance or PRO.

FREE AI must remain bounded by per-player, network and deployment-wide limits. The global budget must be server-controlled and adjustable without redeploying; a conservative initial cap is not a permanent growth ceiling.

**Why:** The owner explicitly rejected a hard-coded global ceiling of 30 FREE scans/day, not budget protection itself.

**How to apply:** Preserve the starting personal quota of three daily scans and independent historical/paid PRO rights. Missing anti-abuse configuration must fail closed, never enable unlimited scans.
