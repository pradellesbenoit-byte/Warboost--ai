Players on every Last War server/alliance, including players without an alliance, must be able to use personal FREE functions after email confirmation and privacy consent, without a beta code, sponsor, invitation or administrator approval.

**Why:** The owner explicitly replaced the private-beta access policy and wants autonomous player registration. This supersedes the alliance-required part of the older onboarding policy.

**How to apply:** Keep personal account access separate from alliance membership, managerial authority and PRO. Preserve historical beta PRO grants, saved data and sessions; never grant PRO or R4/R5 simply by making FREE access available. Alliance membership is scoped by server and tag, with verified identity/manager approval. A self-declared rank or self-authored roster is not independent proof for bootstrapping ownership. Payments remain OFF/TEST; the planned monthly price is not a sale authorization.
