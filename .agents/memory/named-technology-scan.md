---
name: Named Technology scan evidence
description: Why Technology OCR needs card-bound title evidence and independent branch freshness.
---

Treat each visible Technology card as a named, reviewable fact. Percentages require same-card value evidence; explicit max/locked labels are valid text states without a numeric value, but still require a reliable title and same-card state evidence when supplied. Keep uncertain identity or contradictory evidence pending for manual correction rather than mapping it to a generic legacy metric. Max and locked are distinct from numeric 100% and 0%; a displayed 0% remains valid.

**Why:** The old five-field OCR contract put correctly read percentages under unrelated generic labels, while numeric-evidence gating discarded visibly read text-only states. Positional inference and missing-state defaults can make plausible but false progression.

**How to apply:** When evolving Technology scanning or advice, maintain an independent identity and confirmation time for each branch, preserve partial captures and newer confirmed readings, allow explicit card-bound text states without inventing a percentage, and never backfill legacy category percentages from a named card without separate evidence.
