---
name: Named Technology scan evidence
description: Why Technology OCR needs card-bound title evidence and independent branch freshness.
---

Treat each visible Technology card as a named, reviewable fact. A percentage or max/locked state belongs to a branch only when the title and value are both visibly associated with the same card. Keep an uncertain name or value pending for manual correction rather than mapping it to a generic legacy metric. Max and locked are distinct from numeric 100% and 0%; a displayed 0% remains valid.

**Why:** The old five-field OCR contract put correctly read percentages under unrelated generic labels. Positional inference and missing-state defaults can make plausible but false progression.

**How to apply:** When evolving Technology scanning or advice, maintain an independent identity and confirmation time for each branch, preserve partial captures and newer confirmed readings, and never backfill legacy category percentages from a named card without separate evidence.
