## Fetched source 1 — Canyon Storm
URL: https://lastwarhandbook.com/guides/canyon-storm-battlefield-guide
Kind: Independent community strategy guide, not publisher/game documentation.
Fetch states “As of the May 2026 update” and expressly says the mode is evolving/limited testing and many values come from community reports requiring in-game verification [L11, L33, L68, L88, L124].
- Format: 2v1; one Rulebringers alliance versus two Dawnbreakers alliances; cap 20 players per alliance [L27, L49, L71].
- Match duration: approximately 30 minutes [L82, L249].
- Claimed scoring/objective priority: Virus Lab opens about 12 minutes in and has highest point yield; Power Tower second; Data Centers, Defense Systems, Serum Factories and Power Stations at 20 points/sec each; Sample Warehouses 15 points/sec [L51, L86, L115-L120, L161-L167]. Guide flags point values (including +120/sec for Virus Lab) as based on a single community source (gamoria.com), not official documentation [L88, L110, L124].
- Faction mechanics: Rulebringers’ Judicator skill is described with 300s cooldown/120s duration; Dawnbreakers get Artillery Turret; claimed 6,000 base durability [L78, L98, L104, L135, L146, L150].
- Roster substitutions: fetched guide says it does not detail substitutions during an active match.
- Caveat: guide says duration is inferred from timeline, not explicit game specs [L82, L249].

## Fetched source 2 — Desert Storm
URL: https://www.lastwartutorial.com/desert-storm
Kind: Community guide; the fetch explicitly identifies the content as community material [L1, L980].
- Format/cap: two task forces, A and B; up to 60 members per alliance, 20 starters and 10 substitutes per task force [L56, L60, L506, L596].
- Substitution: substitutes may join after battle begins (Stage 1) if a slot is available; an active player must leave through the scoreboard to free a slot [L645, L725, L735-L738].
- Faction colors: own alliance blue, opponent red [L100, L631].
- Timers/objectives: 5-minute preparation; Stage 1 priorities include Oil Refineries, Field Hospitals, Info Center (+10% points) and Science Hub; Nuclear Silo opens at 10 minutes, Oil Wells at 13 minutes [L189, L631-L645, L678-L693, L707].
- Scoring: distinguishes battlefield win points (buildings, supply boxes, gathering) from individual tier-reward points (captures/kills); 40% generation becomes plunderable after holding a building for 60 seconds [L75, L80, L89, L94, L359, L400].
- Version caveat: author says guide revised with event information in May 2025 [L994]; author also acknowledges strategy sections need revision [L963, L994].

## Assessment
These sources provide independent community corroboration of a number of claims, but no primary in-game/publisher evidence was accessed for current rules. The two Desert Storm sources may repeat a shared rules corpus and are not necessarily independent in the evidentiary sense. The numeric differences across event versions are not resolvable from these pages alone. Do not present these community numbers as official/current without in-game verification or accessible publisher documentation.
