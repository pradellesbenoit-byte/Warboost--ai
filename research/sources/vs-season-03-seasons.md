The recurring weekly seasonal activities and event cycles include:

*   **Server vs Server (SvS):** A biweekly event where servers are matched against one another. It features a prep phase (Sunday–Friday) to earn score and invasion rights, followed by a Saturday capital battle known as "Kill Day," where scores are multiplied [L38, L40].
*   **Exclusive Weapons:** New UR exclusive weapons for tank, air, and missile troops are released in weeks 1, 3, and 6 of each season [L46, L48].
*   **Alliance Duel / Weekly Events:** These feature daily themes where players must perform specific actions (building, research, hero upgrades, etc.) on designated days to earn points. Performing actions outside of the assigned daily theme results in zero points [L54, L56].
*   **Awakening (Season 6+):** New to Season 6, this occurs in weeks 1, 3, and 5 for specific heroes, supported by Global Expedition events in weeks 2, 4, and 6 [L50, L52].
