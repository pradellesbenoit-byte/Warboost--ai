# [Introduction](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#introduction) Click to copy link

The Alliance Duel VS is a challenge against an alliance from another server (or from the same server at lower levels). Spanning 6 days from Monday to Saturday, each day features a unique challenge where participants engage in activities to accumulate daily points, contributing to victory points.

Emerging victorious in daily challenges earns not only daily rewards but also culminates in final rewards. Additionally, individual performance during this challenge grants access to chests containing rewards and Valor badges.

These badges facilitate research development aimed at enhancing rewards and points for the Alliance Duel VS, obtainable solely through chest victories in the competition or purchase with cash.

Subsequent messages will delve into the specifics of each day. Note that the points awarded for each activity may vary slightly based on researches you conducted in the Technology Center’s “Alliance Duel” section.

To optimize rewards, it’s crucial to synchronize Alliance Duel VS actions with corresponding phases of the [Arms Race](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/arms-race/ "Arms race – Daily challenge with numerous rewards"). Avoid the urge to execute all actions at the Alliance Duel VS daily reset; each day will specify overlapping [Arms Race](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/arms-race/ "Arms race – Daily challenge with numerous rewards") phases.

**ATTENTION: During server reset, for 5 minutes points are not awarded, first make sure the new duels vs day started going to the event detail page and only after that start doing the activities.**

### [Strategic Preparation](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#strategic-preparation) Click to copy link

Certain activities grant points upon completion, enabling you to initiate them the day before they are required and finish them during their respective phase to maximize points:

- For the days in which radar mission are rewarded, [stack the missions](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/basics/#stack-radar-missions "Basics – Learn about resources, buildings, level ups to be the best player in Last War: Survival Game") to the maximum the day before
- For the days in which gathering food/iron/gold reward points, start the gathering the day before making sure it will end in the new day (before starting the gathering it indicates how much it will take)
- During the days when upgrading buildings rewards points based on their power increase, it’s beneficial to upgrade numerous buildings beforehand in previous days and leave them in a state of gift wrapping. This is because building power is only boosted upon opening the gift package and also duel vs points are given at that moment.
- For days in which research rewards point start a long research the day(s) before so that the research ends in those days. The points are rewarded when clicking on the completed research icon.
- Don’t open Drone Data Chip and Components chest when they are in inventory, as in day 1 and 3 those activities make you gain points.

### [Enemy Buster preparation](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#enemy-buster-preparation) Click to copy link

The last day of the duel rewards points for killing troops of your opponents, so it is important to prepare for this day.

#### [Offense preparation](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#offense-preparation) Click to copy link

- During the week try to upgrade barracks to the highest level possible to unlock training of higher level troops
- Train as many troops as you can, as you will need them during the war. The best would be to use your training speed ups during Day 5: Total Mobilization to maximize points.
- Buy training speed ups and healing speed ups

#### [Defense preparation](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#defense-preparation) Click to copy link

- Buy shields (or spare resources to buy them) to use for the 24 hours of the last day. (Else you will need 1500 diamonds for 8-hour shield!!!)
- Remove troops from your defense lineup, so that even if you get hit you will lose a small amount of troops compared to how much you lose if you have defense up and get defeated: go in your base, click on the wall, click on ![Defense Lineup](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot-from-2024-03-25-23-18-07.png), remove the checks on the squads to remove them from the defense lineup.

## [Rewards](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#rewards) Click to copy link

Each required activity during the duel earns you a designated number of points. You can enhance this point yield by conducting research in the “Alliance Duel” section. However, conducting such researches necessitates the use of Valor badges, which, in turn, can only be acquired by opening chests in the duel.

### [Reward types](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#reward-types) Click to copy link

There are available 3 types of rewards in these event:

- Daily individual reward chests (some of which must be unlocked with researches to be available)
- Daily individual ranking rewards
- Daily Victory rewards (alliance)
- Weekly Tier Rewards for both victory and defeat

All rewards are based on the actual age of the server you are playing in, so don’t expect the pictures below to show the accurate rewards.

## [Alliance Duel Research](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#alliance-duel-research) Click to copy link

To accumulate a substantial number of points, allowing you to unlock chests and compete for victory in the daily duel, you can engage in **Alliance Duel** Researches.

These researches can unlock additional chests (only 3 are available at the beginning, then 2 researches unlock 3 additional chests each), and can increase the point for the activities.

![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/image-270.png)

A good strategy is to use the Valor badges immediately after opening the chests, sparing some of the activities for which you will first increase the point boost in the same day.

## [Task list](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#day-1-task-list) Click to copy link

- **Complete 1 radar task** (~25,000 points):

Go to the radar symbol in the bottom left and enter the radar screen. Here you will see missions, some already completed (with a red dot at the top right) , others still to be completed. To complete a mission you must first execute it (if not already executed) and then click on the mission with the red dot, with this action you collect it and get the points. To maximize points consider stacking missions the day before as explained in [this radar guide](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/radar-missions/ "")

![radar](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Screenshot-from-2024-02-26-16-34-21.png)
- **Use 1 stamina** (~300 points):

Use stamina to make attacks, rallies and to perform radar missions.

![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/stamina.png)
- **Use at least 660 Hero EXP points** at a time (~6 points):

Use experience points, i.e. use the “Upgrade” button after selecting a hero

![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/hero-exp-purple.png)
- **Use 10 Drone Combat Data points** :

Go to the **Drone Center and click the Data Training** button to use battle data. At certain levels it is necessary to have drone parts in order to proceed with training.

![drone battle data](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/drone-battle-data.png)
- **Use 1 Drone Part**:

Go to the **Drone Center** and click the **Data Training** button to use battle data. At certain levels drone parts will be used.

![drone part](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/drone-part.png)
- **Gather 100/100/60 units of food/iron/coins** (~40 points):

Enter map mode, click on the magnifying glass, click on collect, select “Farmland”/”Iron Mine”/”Gold Mine” and find one to send your troops to for gathering

The points are awarded when the troops leave the collection point because the resources to be extracted have run out or because the troops have been recalled. Therefore, consider starting the collection the day before the start of this day making sure that the final collection time is after the start of this day.

![gold mine map](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/gold-mine-map.png)![iron mine map](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/iron-mine-map.png)![food field map](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/food-field-map.png)
- **For every Chip Chest you open, you’ll get Premium chip material x1**  (~2000 points):

Receive points for when you open Drone Data Chip chests from inventory (to not be confused with Drone Component chests). This is available only starting from day 85 of the server when drone skill chip unlocks. With the revamp of the Drone Skill system you also gain a Premmium chip material for each opened chest.

![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/drone-chip-chest-epic.png)![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/drone-chip-chest-rare.png)![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/drone-chip-chest-common.png)![](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/drone-chip-chest-legendary.png)

## [Task list](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/duel-vs/\#day-2-task-list) Click to copy link

- **Use 1-min Construction Speedup** (~120 points):

Use construction speedups (generic ones are fine too, any length).

![speedup builder 3h](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/speedup-builder-5m.png)![speedup generic 3h](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/speedup-generic-3h.png)
- **Increases building power by 1 point** (~21 points):

Every time you complete a building, that building will have increased its power and you get points for that difference. Note that the building’s strength increase only occurs when you open the gift box in which the newly upgraded building is packaged.
- **Dispatch Legendary Trade Truck 1 Time** (~200,000 points):

Trade trucks can be of different types, and consequently have different rewards inside. You can change their content using the **Trade Contract**![trade contract](https://www.lastwartutorial.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/trade-contract.png) . To use them go to the section to send a truck, and when you preview their contents in the top right there is a button with a dice. To get the “Legendary” version you have to update many times until you get the yellow background and the **UR** writing on the left.
