- Linked-account WarBoost management permissions are updated for R4 transitions and rolled back if persistence fails.
- The member rows and grade counters are redrawn immediately after confirmation.
- A cloud sync runs after persistence, then the UI is redrawn again.
- No Supabase migration is required.
- Serverless function count remains unchanged: 12.
