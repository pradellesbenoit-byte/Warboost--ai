DECISION ALIGNMENT RULE
-----------------------
- The Shop engine receives the Diagnostic PRO TOP 3 resource families.
- Matching the first Diagnostic resource family raises shop priority.
- If the same resource family appears at least twice in the TOP 3, it becomes dominant and receives an additional alignment bonus.
- Other scarce progression families receive only a small prudence penalty; they are not deleted and can return to #1 as soon as the account bottleneck changes.
- Live availability remains separate from strategic priority: dated reference items still require verification in Last War.
