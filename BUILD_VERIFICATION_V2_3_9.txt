V2.3.9 TIMING RULE
------------------
- Never point to an already-passed VS day as if it were still ahead in the current cycle.
- If the ideal VS day is ahead: hold for that day in the current cycle.
- If the ideal VS day is today: spend_now.
- If the ideal VS day already passed: point to the next cycle and keep a ROI/Season exception.
